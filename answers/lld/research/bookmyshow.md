## Primary-source facts used on the page
- **Stripe** (https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests, V): Stripe saves the first result for a key "regardless of whether it succeeds or fails", including 500 errors; it may prune keys after 24 hours; a request that clashes with one still running is not saved. So with Stripe, a declined attempt replays as declined under the same key, and a retry with another card needs a new key.
- **Razorpay Orders** (https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/orders/, V): an order "Combines multiple payment attempts for a single order", with a "Single successful payment bound to an order. Prevents multiple payments", and "No further payment requests are allowed once the order moves to the `paid` state".
- The page's contract ("at most one successful charge per key; a declined key may be tried again") is the Razorpay order model, with the booking id as the order. The page names no provider, so no claim depends on either one.
- Not confirmed, so not on the page: BookMyShow's real ticket limit per booking and the length of its hold timer (bookmyshow.com could not be fetched).
- A "no single-seat gap" rule exists in some cinema software, but no interview report asks it; not added.

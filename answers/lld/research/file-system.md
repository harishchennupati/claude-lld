# In-memory file system: what interviewers actually ask (research for the 2026-09-26 review)

Sources: LeetCode Discuss posts read first-hand through the site's API (marked FH); PracHub, HelloInterview, fastprep,
inbyte and darkinterview pages (aggregated or rewritten by the site, marked AGG); plus `landscape-bigtech.json`,
`landscape-india-sea.md` and `landscape-ailabs.md` in this folder. Glassdoor (403), Blind and Reddit were not reachable,
and LeetCode company tags are premium, so they are not used as evidence. Card numbers refer to page 05; tests to FailureTests.java.

Priority for him: Indian product companies and startups, FAANG in India, GCCs / US companies in India, UK and Singapore
companies hiring from India. Reports from India offices: Uber Bangalore SE-2 (cd with `*`), Microsoft Hyderabad L62
(cd with `~`), Amazon Hyderabad SDE2, Adobe MTS-2, Tekion, Freshworks, Allen Digital. Rows seen only at AI labs
(Anthropic/OpenAI/Perplexity) are low priority and drove no change beyond what India/FAANG reports already asked for.
